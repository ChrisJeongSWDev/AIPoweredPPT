All three source files under `.editwork/tasks/AI_Powered_PPT/src/aippt/canvas/` are updated, and the change-detail doc is written to `tasks/AI_Powered_PPT/phase6_backend_change_detail.md`.

Summary of what was done, per the brief:

1. **`style.py`** — added `_split_alpha()`/`apply_alpha()` to parse an `#RRGGBBAA` trailing alpha byte and inject `<a:alpha val="...">` into the DrawingML color element (`a:srgbClr`), wired into both `apply_fill()` and `apply_line()`. Note: I placed `<a:alpha>` inside the `a:srgbClr` element rather than as a direct child of `a:solidFill` as the brief literally described — that's the ECMA-376-correct location; appending it directly under `solidFill` is well-formed XML that PowerPoint silently ignores.
2. **`app.py`** — bumped `MAX_BODY_BYTES` to 50MB and rewrote the JSON `pptx`-export endpoint to accept `multipart/form-data`, reading IR JSON from a form field `"ir"` and saving uploaded media to a `tempfile.mkdtemp()` directory, passed to `render()` as `media_map`, cleaned up in a `finally` block.
3. **`render_canvas.py`** — `render()` now accepts `media_map`, threaded through to new `draw_image`/`draw_video` functions that call `add_picture`/`add_movie` per the brief.
