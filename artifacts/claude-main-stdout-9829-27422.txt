## One design decision worth recording

Requirement 3 says the pin must reflect observed reality, never a guess. Since the builder cannot be executed here, the honest shape of the deliverable is: `make_renderer_template.py` emits `template_pin.json` **entirely from inspection of the built package**, and the committed `template_pin.json` ships in an explicitly *unpinned* state (`"pinned": false`, `renderer_template.sha256: null`) so that `pin.py` fails closed until the builder has actually run. The renderer template's SHA is unknowable without building it, and ZIP timestamps make rebuilds non-reproducible anyway — so there is no honest way to pre-fill it.
