 281: ### 5.2 Canvas sizing and the coordinate system
 282: 
 283: **The model space is 960 횞 540 logical units, and nothing else ever enters the IR.**
 284:

 353: ### 5.4 Shapes: inline SVG, because SVG stroke semantics == DrawingML stroke semantics
 354: 
 355: Critic rule 8 demands we **define whether stroke is included in `w`/`h`**. We define it by construction:

 592: ### 6.6 Text frames ??the margin trap, and the truth about autofit
 593: 
 594: **The trap nobody writes down: text-frame internal margins.** PowerPoint defaults to 0.1 in left/right and 0.05 in top/bottom (91,440 / 45,720 EMU) ??**7.2 and 3.6 logical units**.
