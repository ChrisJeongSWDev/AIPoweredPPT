## Three judgement calls worth your attention

1. **The §5.6 CSS contract has a latent bug I had to work around.** It puts `display: flex` on `.el .txt` and maps `align-items`→valign, `justify-content`→align. That mapping is only correct with *exactly one flex item* — a contenteditable holding several paragraphs produces several block children, which as direct flex items lay out **side by side in a row**. I kept the §5.6 declarations verbatim and gave `.txt` a single `.body` child that is the editable surface. Contract preserved, multi-paragraph text works. This is my one structural departure from the brief's HTML sketch, and it's forced.
2. **A trailing `<br>` (last child of its block) is dropped** — Chrome inserts one into every empty contenteditable block. Emitting it would add a phantom line to the PPTX. Cost: a Shift+Enter at the very end of a text box doesn't survive the round trip. Reversible if you disagree.
3. **`test_app.py` *skips* rather than fails without the editor extra**, so `pytest tests/` stays green for a renderer-only install. That means Gate 2 can look green having checked nothing — when you run it, confirm the summary says **passed**, not **skipped**.
